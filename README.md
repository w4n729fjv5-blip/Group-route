# Keyfall

A piano practice app for iPad. It listens to your piano through the microphone and teaches songs in two views: falling notes and sheet music.

Open it at https://w4n729fjv5-blip.github.io/Group-route/ in Safari, tap **Listen to my piano**, and allow the microphone.

This branch (`gh-pages`) only holds the app. The rest of Group-route lives on its other branches and is unchanged.
