# Keyfall

A piano practice app for iPad. It listens to your piano through the microphone and teaches songs in two views: falling notes and sheet music.

Open it at https://w4n729fjv5-blip.github.io/Group-route/ in Safari, tap **Listen to my piano**, and allow the microphone.

This branch (`gh-pages`) only holds the app. The rest of Group-route lives on its other branches and is unchanged.

## Keystone

A second app lives in `keystone/`: piano fundamentals for iPad, with lessons on hand technique, ear training, reading music, scales, keys and chords, plus falling-block exercises and a sampled grand piano (Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0).

Open it at https://w4n729fjv5-blip.github.io/Group-route/keystone/ . To use a MIDI keyboard on iPad, open that link in MIDIWeb Browser and tap **Connect keyboard**.
